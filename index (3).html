Platinum Jubilee Celebration

Dear Esteemed Alumni, Greetings from the Platinum Jubilee Committee of Alagappa Chettiar Government College of Engineering & Technology (ACCET), Karaikudi As ACCET proudly celebrates 75 years of excellence, we honor a rich legacy of academic achievement, innovation, and service established by our founder, Dr. Alagappa Chettiar. For generations, the institution has shaped accomplished engineers, entrepreneurs, academicians, and leaders who have made a lasting impact across the world. To mark this historic milestone, the Platinum Jubilee Committee has launched an initiative to strengthen and modernize the college's infrastructure, including academic facilities, laboratories, and student amenities. Your generous support will help create a better learning environment for future generations and ensure that ACCET's legacy of excellence continues for years to come. Together, let us celebrate our proud heritage by investing in the future of our beloved alma mater.

Founder: Padma Bhushan Alagappa Chettiar

Alagappa Chettiar, born in 1909 into a prominent Chettiar family in Karaikudi, Tamil Nadu, left an indelible mark on the region's economic and educational landscape. A successful businessman, he expanded his family's enterprises, including banking and finance. However, his most enduring legacy lies in education and philanthropy. In 1950, he established the Alagappa Chettiar Educational Trust, dedicated to promoting education and research. The trust's flagship institution, Alagappa Chettiar College of Engineering and Technology, stands as a beacon of engineering education in Tamil Nadu, contributing significantly to technological advancements in the state. Beyond education, Alagappa Chettiar actively supported healthcare, community development, and social welfare initiatives, contributing to the holistic development of the region. His unwavering commitment to education and philanthropy earned him recognition and honors, and his legacy continues to inspire those dedicated to advancing education and societal progress in Tamil Nadu and beyond.
